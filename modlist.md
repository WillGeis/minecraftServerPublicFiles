# Mod List (Work in Progress)

#### all were downloaded with settings 1.20.1 and fabric

## World Generation / Biomes
- **Biomes O’ Plenty** – Expands world generation with dozens of new biomes, trees, plants, and blocks.  
- **BetterEnd** – Overhauls the End dimension with new biomes, blocks, mobs, and progression.  
- **BetterNether** – Expands the Nether with new biomes, plants, mobs, and structures.  
- **Dungeons and Taverns** – Adds large, detailed structures like castles, dungeons, and adventure sites.  
- **Sparse Structures** – Adds scattered small structures to diversify exploration.  
- **YUNG’s API** – Core library for YUNG’s worldgen mods.  
- **YUNG’s Bridges** – Adds naturally spawning bridges across rivers, canyons, and ravines.  
- **TerraBlender** – Library for biome and dimension mod compatibility.  

## Building & Decoration
- **Chisel (Fabric)** – Adds a chisel tool to carve blocks into decorative variations.  
- **Create: Structures** – Adds premade structures themed around the Create mod.  
- **Create Deco** – Decorative building blocks that integrate with Create.  
- **Toomanypaintings** – Adds many new paintings for decoration.  
- **Supplementaries** – Adds functional & decorative blocks (lanterns, jars, traps, signs).  
- **Artifacts** – Adds unique equipment and curiosities with special powers.  
- **Travelers’ Titles** – Adds screen titles when entering new locations (like biomes/structures).  

## Technology & Machines
- **Create (Fabric)** – The main tech mod with kinetic machinery, conveyor belts, automation.  
- **Create: Addition** – Adds electricity, batteries, and electrical devices to Create.  
- **Create: Jetpack** – Jetpack powered by Create’s resources.  
- **Create: Ore Excavation** – Adds automated mining drills and excavation machines.  
- **Create: Big Cannons** – Adds massive artillery and siege weapons using Create’s systems.  
- **Steam Rails** – Adds a fully functional railway system with trains, tracks, and signaling.  

## Farming & Food
- **Farmer’s Delight (Refabricated)** – Adds cooking, crops, and farming utilities.  
- **Slice and Dice** – Expands cooking with new food prep tools for Farmer’s Delight.  
- **Delightful Chefs** – Adds chefs and villagers related to cooking mods.  
- **Delightful Creators** – Expands cooking compatibility with Create.  
- **End’s Delight (Refabricated)** – Adds End dimension foods and recipes.  
- **Ocean’s Delight** – Adds seafood-based recipes.  

## Exploration & Utility
- **Artifacts** – Exploration-based artifacts that give abilities and effects.  
- **Natures Compass** – Lets you locate specific biomes.  
- **Traveler’s Backpack** – Wearable backpacks with storage and upgrades.  
- **Magic Mirror** – A teleportation item that returns you to your spawn/bed.  
- **Immersive Aircraft** – Adds flyable aircraft for exploration.  
- **Immersive Armors** – Expands armor variety with new sets and models.  
- **Villagers Plus** – Adds more villager professions and variety.  
- **Gravestones** – Places a grave with your items when you die.  
- **Xaero’s Minimap** – Mini-map for navigation.  
- **Xaero’s World Map** – Fullscreen world map integration.  

## Performance, Fixes & Core Libraries
- **Fabric API** – Core API for most Fabric mods.  
- **Fabric Language Kotlin** – Kotlin support for Fabric mods.  
- **Architectury API** – Cross-loader modding API for Fabric/Forge.  
- **Cardinal Components API** – Data storage system for mods.  
- **Fusion** – Core mod library (dependency).  
- **Moonlight (Selene)** – Library for mods like Supplementaries.  
- **GlitchCore** – Core mod for certain features.  
- **Cryonic Config** – Configuration library.  
- **Attribute Fix** – Fixes attribute system limits (like health/damage).  
- **Memory Leak Fix** – Prevents memory leak issues with entities and chunks.  
- **FerriteCore** – Performance optimization for memory usage.  
- **Sodium** – Rendering performance boost.  
- **Indium** – Adds missing features (Fabric rendering API) for Sodium.  
- **Lithostitched** – Fixes resource pack conflicts with stone variants.  

## Visual & Cosmetic
- **AppleSkin** – Shows hunger/saturation info in the HUD.  
- **Clumps** – Groups XP orbs into stacks for less lag.  
- **Fallingleaves** – Adds falling leaf particles.  
- **LambDynamicLights** – Entities/items emit real-time light.  
- **Visuality** – Particle and visual enhancements.  
- **Skin Layers 3D** – Adds 3D rendering to player’s second skin layer.  
- **Eating Animation** – Adds animations when eating/drinking.  
- **Elytra Slot** – Adds a separate slot for Elytra (chestplate compatibility).  
- **Trinkets** – Extra equipment slots for accessories.  
- **More Mob Variants** – Adds visual variety to mobs.  
- **Naturalist** – Adds wildlife, animals, and nature immersion.  
- **Amendments** – Visual and immersive tweaks to improve world detail.  
- **CTOV (Connected Textures)** – Adds connected textures & visual enhancements.  
- **Not Enough Animations** – Brings back third-person animations like swinging, shield blocking, etc.  
- **Mouse Tweaks** – Improves inventory management with better mouse controls.  
- **Mod Menu** – Menu to view/manage mods in-game.  
- **Jade** – Shows tooltips with block/entity info (WAILA-style).  
- **Just Enough Items (JEI)** – Recipe viewing system.
